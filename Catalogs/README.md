# 📖 Catalogs

This section contains original Toyota Paseo catalogs, brochures, accessory guides,
and other dealership materials from the vehicle's production years.

These documents offer a glimpse into how the Toyota Paseo was presented to
customers, showcasing available trims, factory options, accessories, colors,
features, and period-correct configurations.

---

## Contents

- Sales brochures
- Accessory catalogs
- Color charts
- Price lists
- Dealer promotional material

---

## Preservation Project

Many of these documents have become difficult to find over time. The goal of this
archive is to preserve and share them with the Toyota Paseo community for
historical, educational, and enthusiast purposes.

If you own catalogs or promotional material that are not yet included, please
consider contributing to help expand the archive.
