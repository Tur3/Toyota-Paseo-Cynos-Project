# 🔧 Upgrades & Modifications

This section collects technical documents, compatibility lists, and reference guides exploring upgrade and modification possibilities for the **Toyota Paseo**.

The goal is to help owners discover compatible components from other vehicles, explore potential upgrades, and find useful alternatives to original parts that may be difficult to source today.

## 📂 Contents

This section may include:

- **Parts Compatibility Lists** – Components from other Toyota models or vehicles that may be compatible with the Paseo.
- **Upgrade Possibilities** – Potential improvements using parts from other vehicles.
- **Cross-Reference Guides** – Technical references to help identify alternative components and replacement parts.
- **Modification Resources** – Documents covering possible mechanical, electrical, and other vehicle modifications.

## ⚠️ Important Notes

Compatibility information should always be verified before purchasing or installing any component. Parts that appear similar or share a reference number may still require modifications or additional components.

Always check dimensions, mounting points, electrical connections, mechanical specifications, and vehicle-specific requirements. Modifications affecting safety or road legality should be assessed carefully and comply with applicable regulations.

The documents in this section are provided for informational and research purposes. Inclusion in this archive does not guarantee direct compatibility or plug-and-play installation.
